honest and direct, without pretense.

“The interviewer asked for a candid answer, not a rehearsed one.”

meticulous medium

showing great attention to detail; very careful.

“Her meticulous preparation impressed the entire panel.”

Synonyms: thorough, precise   Opposites: careless, sloppy

ambiguous hard

open to more than one interpretation; unclear.

“His ambiguous response left the interviewer unsure of his intent.”
