Financial Fraud Investigation vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Financial Fraud Investigation” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

embezzle medium

to steal money entrusted to one's care, usually from an employer.

“Auditors discovered he had embezzled nearly two million dollars over six years.”

Synonyms: misappropriate, steal funds   Opposites: safeguard, account honestly

laundering medium

the process of disguising the origins of illegally obtained money.

“The shell companies existed solely for laundering illicit funds.”

Synonyms: money cleaning, fund disguising   Opposites: transparent accounting, legitimate transfer

collusion hard

secret cooperation for a deceitful or illegal purpose.

“Investigators suspected collusion between the broker and the accountant.”

Synonyms: conspiracy, secret agreement   Opposites: independence, honest competition

insolvent medium

unable to pay debts owed.

“The company had been insolvent for months before anyone admitted it publicly.”

Synonyms: bankrupt, broke   Opposites: solvent, financially stable

fraudulent easy

obtained or done through deception, especially for financial gain.

“The fraudulent invoices had passed unnoticed for nearly three years.”

Synonyms: deceptive, dishonest   Opposites: legitimate, honest

whistleblower easy

a person who exposes wrongdoing within an organization.

“It was a junior whistleblower who finally brought the scheme to light.”

Synonyms: informant, tipster   Opposites: accomplice, enabler

restitution medium

the restoration of something lost or stolen to its rightful owner.

“The court ordered full restitution to every affected investor.”

Synonyms: repayment, compensation   Opposites: deprivation, loss

subpoena medium

a legal order requiring someone to attend court or produce evidence.

“Three executives received a subpoena within the same week.”

Synonyms: legal summons, court order   Opposites:

indictment hard

a formal accusation of a serious crime.

“The indictment listed over forty separate counts of fraud.”

Synonyms: formal charge, accusation   Opposites: acquittal, exoneration

audit easy

an official inspection of financial accounts.

“A routine audit was all it took to unravel the entire scheme.”

Synonyms: financial review, inspection   Opposites:
