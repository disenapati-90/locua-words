Adoption Process vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Adoption Process” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

guardianship medium

the position of being legally responsible for someone unable to care for themselves.

“Temporary guardianship was granted while the paperwork was finalized.”

Synonyms: custodianship, legal care   Opposites:

biological easy

related by blood rather than by adoption or marriage.

“She met her biological mother for the first time at twenty-six.”

Synonyms: birth-related, genetic   Opposites: adoptive, foster

eligibility medium

the state of meeting requirements needed to qualify for something.

“Proving eligibility took nearly as long as the adoption itself.”

Synonyms: qualification, suitability   Opposites: ineligibility, disqualification

bureaucracy medium

a system of administration marked by complex procedures and rules.

“They hadn't expected quite so much bureaucracy standing between them and their daughter.”

Synonyms: red tape, administrative process   Opposites: simplicity, efficiency

unconditional easy

not subject to any conditions; complete.

“From the first meeting, her love for him felt entirely unconditional.”

Synonyms: absolute, unreserved   Opposites: conditional, qualified

legitimize hard

to make something legally or socially acceptable.

“The final court hearing would legitimize what already felt true in their hearts.”

Synonyms: legalize, validate   Opposites: invalidate, delegitimize

kinship medium

a family relationship or blood relation.

“A distant kinship connection made the placement easier to arrange.”

Synonyms: family bond, relation   Opposites: estrangement, unrelatedness

reunification hard

the act of bringing people back together after separation.

“Reunification with the birth family was never part of their plan, but it happened anyway.”

Synonyms: reuniting, coming back together   Opposites: separation, estrangement

nurturing easy

caring for and encouraging the growth of someone.

“The home study praised their calm, nurturing approach to parenting.”

Synonyms: caring, supportive   Opposites: neglectful, indifferent

belonging easy

the feeling of being accepted as part of a group or place.

“For the first time, he said, he finally felt a sense of belonging.”

Synonyms: acceptance, inclusion   Opposites: alienation, exclusion
