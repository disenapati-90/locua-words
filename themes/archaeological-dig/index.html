excavate easy

to dig up and expose something buried, especially of historical interest.

“The team spent three seasons carefully excavating the buried temple floor.”

Synonyms: dig up, unearth   Opposites: bury, cover

an object made by a human, especially of historical interest.

“Each artifact was photographed and logged before being moved an inch.”

“The tablet offered rare insight into daily life in antiquity.”
