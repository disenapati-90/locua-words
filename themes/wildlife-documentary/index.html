Wildlife Documentary vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Wildlife Documentary” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

predatory easy

relating to an animal that naturally preys on others.

“The narrator described the predatory instincts of the lioness as she stalked the herd.”

Synonyms: carnivorous, hunting   Opposites: docile, harmless

camouflage easy

coloring or pattern that allows an animal to blend into its surroundings.

“Its camouflage was so effective that the crew almost missed the shot entirely.”

Synonyms: disguise, concealment   Opposites: exposure, visibility

migratory medium

relating to animals that move seasonally between regions.

“The migratory birds traveled thousands of miles without a single rest stop.”

Synonyms: nomadic, wandering   Opposites: sedentary, resident

symbiotic medium

involving mutual benefit between two different organisms.

“The clownfish and anemone share a symbiotic relationship that benefits them both.”

Synonyms: mutualistic, interdependent   Opposites: parasitic, one-sided

dormant medium

temporarily inactive but capable of becoming active again.

“The volcano had been dormant for centuries before the eruption footage was filmed.”

Synonyms: inactive, quiescent   Opposites: active, erupting

endemic hard

native to and found only in a particular place.

“The species is endemic to a single remote valley in the region.”

Synonyms: native, indigenous   Opposites: widespread, introduced

apex predator hard

a predator at the top of a food chain, with no natural predators of its own.

“As the apex predator of the region, it had virtually no natural threats.”

Synonyms: top predator, dominant hunter   Opposites: prey species, bottom of the chain

biodiversity medium

the variety of plant and animal life in a habitat.

“The reef supported a level of biodiversity rarely seen anywhere else on Earth.”

Synonyms: ecological variety, species richness   Opposites: monoculture, uniformity

extinction easy

the state or process of a species dying out entirely.

“Without intervention, the species faced extinction within a single generation.”

Synonyms: dying out, eradication   Opposites: survival, proliferation

ecosystem easy

a community of living organisms interacting with their environment.

“Removing even one species disrupted the balance of the entire ecosystem.”
