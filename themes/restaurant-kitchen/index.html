reduction medium

a sauce thickened and concentrated by simmering.

“The sauce needed another twenty minutes to become a proper reduction.”

Synonyms: concentrate, thickened sauce   Opposites: dilution, thin liquid

emulsify hard

to combine two liquids that don't normally mix into a stable blend.

“Whisking slowly helped the oil and vinegar emulsify into a smooth dressing.”

Synonyms: blend, combine smoothly   Opposites: separate, curdle

mise en place hard

the practice of organizing and arranging ingredients before cooking.

“Good mise en place meant every station was ready before service began.”

Synonyms: prep setup, organized workstation   Opposites: disorganization, chaos

Synonyms: appetizing, agreeable   Opposites: unpalatable, distasteful

Synonyms: spoiled, sour   Opposites: fresh, wholesome
