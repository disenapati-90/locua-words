Divorce Proceedings vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Divorce Proceedings” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

alimony medium

financial support paid by one spouse to another after separation.

“The court ordered monthly alimony to be paid for the next five years.”

Synonyms: spousal support, maintenance payment   Opposites:

custody easy

the legal right to care for a child, especially after divorce.

“The custody arrangement was finalized only after months of negotiation.”

Synonyms: guardianship, care rights   Opposites:

mediation medium

a process where a neutral third party helps resolve a dispute.

“They opted for mediation instead of a lengthy courtroom battle.”

Synonyms: negotiated settlement, arbitration   Opposites: litigation, confrontation

irreconcilable hard

impossible to resolve or bring into agreement.

“The papers cited irreconcilable differences as the reason for the split.”

Synonyms: incompatible, unresolvable   Opposites: reconcilable, compatible

settlement easy

a formal agreement resolving a dispute.

“The settlement divided their assets almost exactly down the middle.”

Synonyms: agreement, resolution   Opposites: dispute, impasse

estranged medium

no longer close or connected; alienated.

“By the time the papers were signed, they were already estranged in every way that mattered.”

Synonyms: alienated, distant   Opposites: close, reconciled

annulment hard

a legal ruling that a marriage was never valid.

“She sought an annulment rather than a divorce, given the circumstances.”

Synonyms: voiding of marriage, nullification   Opposites:

cordial easy

warm and friendly, though somewhat formal.

“Remarkably, their post-divorce relationship remained cordial for the kids' sake.”

Synonyms: amicable, civil   Opposites: hostile, bitter

litigate medium

to take a dispute to a court of law.

“Neither side wanted to litigate the custody issue in open court.”

Synonyms: take to court, sue   Opposites: settle privately, mediate

closure easy

a feeling of resolution or completion after a difficult experience.

“Signing the final papers gave her a strange, quiet sense of closure.”

Synonyms: resolution, finality   Opposites: unresolved feelings, lingering doubt
