Space Mission vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Space Mission” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

trajectory medium

the path followed by an object moving through space.

“Engineers recalculated the trajectory after the unexpected fuel loss.”

Synonyms: flight path, course   Opposites:

propulsion medium

the force that drives something forward.

“The new propulsion system cut the journey time nearly in half.”

Synonyms: thrust, drive   Opposites: drag, resistance

orbit easy

the curved path of an object around a star, planet, or moon.

“The satellite settled into a stable orbit after a tense hour.”

Synonyms: revolution path, circuit   Opposites:

gravitational medium

relating to the force of attraction between masses.

“The gravitational pull of the moon altered the shuttle's approach angle.”

Synonyms: relating to gravity, attractive force   Opposites:

reentry easy

the return of a spacecraft into a planet's atmosphere.

“Reentry into the atmosphere is one of the most dangerous parts of any mission.”

Synonyms: atmospheric return, descent   Opposites: launch, ascent

extravehicular hard

taking place outside a spacecraft, in open space.

“The astronaut prepared for a six-hour extravehicular repair mission.”

Synonyms: outside the craft, spacewalk-related   Opposites: internal, in-cabin

telemetry hard

data collected remotely and transmitted for monitoring.

“Mission control lost telemetry for nearly ninety terrifying seconds.”

Synonyms: remote data, tracking signal   Opposites:

payload easy

the cargo or equipment carried by a vehicle, especially a spacecraft.

“The rocket's payload included three satellites and a research module.”

Synonyms: cargo, load   Opposites:

docking medium

the act of joining two spacecraft together in orbit.

“Docking with the station required millimeter-precise maneuvering.”

Synonyms: coupling, linking up   Opposites: undocking, separation

anomaly medium

something that deviates from what is standard or expected.

“Engineers spotted an anomaly in the engine readings just before launch.”

Synonyms: irregularity, deviation   Opposites: normalcy, consistency
