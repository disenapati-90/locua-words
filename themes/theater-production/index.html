Theater Production vocabulary

Words you meet in a “Theater Production” story, each with a clear meaning and an example sentence. In the Locua app these are read in story order so the context helps you remember them.

rehearsal easy

a practice session for a performance.

“The final rehearsal ran nearly two hours over schedule.”

Synonyms: run-through, practice session   Opposites:

understudy medium

a performer who learns another's role to be able to replace them.

“The understudy went on with only two hours' notice and never missed a line.”

Synonyms: stand-in, backup performer   Opposites: lead, star

improvise easy

to create or perform something without preparation.

“When the prop broke, she had to improvise the entire scene on the spot.”

Synonyms: ad-lib, extemporize   Opposites: rehearse, script

dramaturgy hard

the study and craft of dramatic composition and theatrical representation.

“The director brought in a dramaturgy consultant to sharpen the script's structure.”

Synonyms: playwriting craft, theatrical composition   Opposites:

blocking medium

the precise staging of actors' movements in a scene.

“The blocking for the final scene took three full rehearsals to finalize.”

Synonyms: stage positioning, movement planning   Opposites:

ensemble easy

a group of performers working together as a whole.

“The ensemble cast made the show feel bigger than any single star.”

Synonyms: cast group, company   Opposites: soloist, individual

denouement hard

the final resolution of a plot in a story or play.

“The denouement tied every subplot together in under five minutes.”

Synonyms: resolution, conclusion   Opposites: climax, rising action

soliloquy hard

an act of speaking one's thoughts aloud alone on stage.

“Her soliloquy in the second act drew a standing ovation on opening night.”

Synonyms: monologue, solo speech   Opposites: dialogue, conversation

critique easy

a detailed analysis or assessment of something.

“The morning critique in the paper was harsher than anyone expected.”

Synonyms: review, evaluation   Opposites: praise, endorsement

curtain call easy

the return of performers to the stage to be applauded after a show.

“The curtain call lasted nearly ten minutes as the audience refused to stop clapping.”

Synonyms: final bow, cast bow   Opposites:
