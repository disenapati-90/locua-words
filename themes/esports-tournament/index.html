Esports Tournament vocabulary

clutch easy

a high-pressure moment where success depends entirely on the individual.

“His clutch play in the final round turned the whole match around.”

Synonyms: pressure moment, decisive play   Opposites: choke, collapse

the most effective tactics or strategies currently favored in a competitive game.

“The team had built their entire strategy around the current meta.”

synergy medium

the combined effect of two things being greater than their separate effects.

“Their draft relied entirely on synergy between two specific characters.”

Synonyms: combined effect, team chemistry   Opposites: mismatch, incompatibility

underdog easy

a competitor expected to lose.

“As the underdog, nobody expected them to reach the grand finals.”

Synonyms: long shot, unlikely winner   Opposites: favorite, frontrunner

stalemate medium

a situation in which no further action can be taken by either side.

“The match reached a stalemate that lasted nearly twenty extra minutes.”

Synonyms: deadlock, standoff   Opposites: breakthrough, decisive advantage

momentum easy

the strength or force gained by a developing situation.

“Once they took the first objective, momentum swung entirely in their favor.”

Synonyms: forward drive, building strength   Opposites: stagnation, loss of drive

coordination easy

the organization of people to work together effectively.

“Their coordination across five players looked almost telepathic by the finals.”

Synonyms: teamwork, synchronization   Opposites: disarray, disorganization

financial support given in exchange for advertising or association.

champion easy

a person who has defeated all rivals in a competition.

“They walked off the stage as world champions, still in disbelief.”

Synonyms: victor, titleholder   Opposites: runner-up, loser
