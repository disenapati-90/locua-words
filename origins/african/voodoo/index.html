voodoo

African (Fon/Ewe)medium

Meaning: a spiritual tradition blending African, Catholic, and Indigenous practices.

Origin: African (Fon/Ewe) — vodun

Did you know? From 'vodun,' meaning 'spirit' in Fon and Ewe languages of West Africa.
